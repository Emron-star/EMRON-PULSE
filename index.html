About EMRON PULSE

This is a client-side statistical scanner for Deriv synthetic indices. It analyses the last N ticks of public tick data. No trade execution, no account access — read-only analysis.

Strategies:

  • Rise / Fall: Directional read on the price sequence — 5/10/20-tick momentum, higher highs & higher lows, short and medium trend, momentum acceleration, and a choppy-regime filter
  • Even/Odd: Bias toward even or odd last digits
  • Over/Under: Bias toward digits ≥5 or <5
  • Transitional Mesh: Locks the digit that stays the most appearing digit across the 150 / 100 / 50 / 25 / 15 lookback windows. A prediction only fires when the 15- and 25-tick windows agree and the digit stays compatible with 50, 100, and 150. The engine then monitors the digit live — blue or white means keep the bot running; red means the window has closed — stop immediately and re-scan.
  • High Tick / Low Tick: Compares the last-digit distribution against a barrier digit. Predicts whether the next last digit will land above (HIGH) or below (LOW) it.
  • Only Ups / Only Downs: Estimates the per-tick up/down bias and compares the probability of a full N-tick consecutive run against the fair-coin baseline of 0.5N.

Transitional Mesh — entry window rules:

  • Conditions to open: The same digit must be a top digit in the 15- and 25-tick windows, and stay compatible with the 50-, 100- and 150-tick windows (top-3 or ≥70% of that window's max count). When all five confirm, that digit is locked in and the ENTRY WINDOW OPENS.
  • While open: The engine keeps checking every tick whether the digit is still the most appearing digit in the 25-tick window. As long as it stays on top, the window remains open and you keep the bot running.
  • Window closes: The moment another digit overtakes your prediction — i.e. it is no longer the most appearing digit — the ENTRY WINDOW CLOSES. Stop immediately and re-scan the mesh for a fresh prediction.
  • After closing, the engine displays the closed state for a short window before looking for a new prediction, so you can see the alert and act on it.
  • Temperature: GREEN = window open & dominant. AMBER = window still open but the digit has tied or gone quiet. RED = window closed.
  • Window-open signals are logged for next-tick resolution (hit if the next last digit equals the prediction).
  • $150 profit-target reminder is displayed; the session counter lets you mark a reset each time you bank a target.

Statistics:

  • Wilson-adjusted z-score: Jeffreys prior for stable estimates on small samples
  • Multi-scale ensemble: blends short, medium, and long windows (digit strategies)
  • Regime filter: lag-1 autocorrelation for digits, efficiency ratio for Rise/Fall
  • Signal confirmation: the direction must persist for several consecutive ticks
  • Signal decay: confidence fades as time passes after the signal
  • Deduplication: prevents repeated alerts on the same move
