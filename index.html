Matches Lab runs nine competing models simultaneously on the same tick stream — Transitional Mesh, Extended Mesh, Frequency Momentum, 3-digit and 4-digit historical sequences, 1st and 2nd order Markov chains, pattern-shape matching, and X2X timing — then combines them with a weighted ensemble. Every model prediction is logged and resolved against the very next tick so the measured hit rate is real, not a score. The break-even probability is computed from the assumed Matches payout so you can see whether the signal actually has positive expected value. Read-only analysis, not trade execution.

About EMRON PULSE

Client-side statistical scanner for Deriv synthetic indices. Read-only analysis of public tick data — no trade execution, no account access.

Matches Lab — models:

  • A · Transitional Mesh: 150/100/50/25/15 lookback consistency
  • B · Extended Mesh: 100/75/50/25/15/10
  • C · Frequency Momentum: digit whose frequency is accelerating as the window shrinks
  • D · Historical Seq 3: next-digit distribution after the last 3 digits
  • E · Historical Seq 4: same with 4 digits
  • F · Markov 1: P(next | last digit)
  • G · Markov 2: P(next | last 2 digits)
  • H · Pattern: AA / AAA / AAB / AABB / ABAB / ABBA shapes → next digit
  • I · X2X Timing: after X?X patterns, which digit tends to appear next
  • J · Ensemble: weighted vote across all models. Signals fire only when the score and the number of agreeing models pass the gate.

Research record: every model prediction and every ensemble signal is logged and resolved against the very next tick. The right-hand panel shows the measured hit rate per model. The break-even probability (1 / payout, default 9.5×) is shown so you can judge whether the ensemble edge is statistically defensible.

Past performance does not guarantee future results. Trade responsibly.
